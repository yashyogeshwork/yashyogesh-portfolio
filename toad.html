Toyota · Concept

TOAD

A farming companion for people who never learned to farm.

Scroll
The problem

Newly rural. Zero farming experience.

Neo-rural
Target generation
Small-plot
Hobby & subsistence farming
The user

Digital work by day, farms by choice.

They moved for space, not to become farmers. TOAD is built for someone with no agricultural background who still wants real results from their land.

The system

Learns your land. Does the heavy work.

It studies the soil, the seasons, and the crops, then does the physical work itself, guiding its owner through decisions instead of expecting them to already know.

Form

Shaped like a toad. Low and grounded.

A low, wide stance and a soft, rounded body, an animal-inspired form built to feel grounded and approachable, not industrial.

Exterior

Built low, wide, and calm.

Every surface reads as calm and close to the ground, built to move through a field without looking like it belongs on a construction site.

Interior

No cockpit. Just the field.

There's no cabin to sit in. TOAD is guided through a simple companion app and on-body signals, the interaction happens in the field itself, not behind a windshield.
